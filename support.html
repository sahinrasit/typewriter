Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to mrrasitsahin@icloud.com. We read everything and usually reply within two working days.

Where are my documents?

Each document is a plain Markdown (.md) file. By default they live in the Files app under On My iPhone › Typewriter (or On My iPad). To write on several devices, open Settings › Choose Folder… in Typewriter and pick a folder in iCloud Drive.

Will I lose my writing if I delete the app?

Documents stored in an iCloud Drive folder you chose stay there. Documents stored “On My iPhone” are removed together with the app, so move or export them first.

What does the free version include?

You can write two documents of up to 500 words each with every writing feature. The one-time purchase unlocks unlimited documents and words, export (Word, PDF, Markdown, RTF, HTML) and printing.

Restoring your purchase

Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy Typewriter. Family Sharing is supported.

Markdown basics

# Heading, **bold**, *italic*, - list, - [ ] task, > quote, [link](https://…), * * * for a scene break. The bar above the keyboard inserts all of these; on a hardware keyboard use ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘K.

Shortcuts and Siri

Typewriter adds “New Document” and “Today’s Word Count” actions to the Shortcuts app.
