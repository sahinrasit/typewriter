Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Typewriter is a writing app. It is designed so that what you write stays in files you control.

Your writing

Documents are plain text files stored on your device or, if you choose, in a folder of your iCloud Drive (synced by Apple under your own iCloud account). We do not upload, read, analyse or back up your writing. Typewriter has no account system and no server of its own. Word counts, goals and settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases

The Typewriter Lifetime unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app verifies purchases with Apple’s StoreKit and may use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to validate and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app, the purchase receipt from Apple, the app version, device model and country or region, used only to provide and restore your purchase. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Children

Typewriter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
